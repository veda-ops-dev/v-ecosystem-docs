# Batch H — Waiting on Provider Credits

## Purpose

This note records a deliberate stop point for the remaining DataForSEO portion of Batch H.

It exists so the repo states plainly that work is paused for evidence reasons, not because the ownership or provider posture is unclear.

---

## Status

Paused pending provider credits.

This is a transition-support control note.
It is not authority doctrine.

---

## What is already settled

The following remain settled enough and should not be reopened because of this pause:

- VEDA is observatory-only
- Firecrawl is an admitted VEDA observatory provider
- DataForSEO AI Optimization is an admitted VEDA observatory provider
- pre-project observability is required
- raw AI-surface observability belongs in VEDA
- derived interpretation belongs in VEDA Strategy
- provider admission does not settle canonical family design
- actual payload structure should outrank design inference when family design is being promoted

---

## Why work is paused

The remaining unresolved DataForSEO AI-surface family questions are now constrained more by missing real payload evidence than by missing design effort.

The repo already has:

- playground-confirmed grounding for earlier DataForSEO work
- official-doc-confirmed structural guidance for unsampled surfaces
- a Batch H design pass and follow-on correction/inventory notes

But the user has explicitly stopped before unlocking the LLM mentions / citation-related surfaces because doing so requires adding provider credits.

That means further promotion of docs-only DataForSEO structures would increase the risk of overclaiming family design beyond the available evidence.

Pausing here is intentional and correct.

---

## What this pause applies to

This pause applies specifically to the DataForSEO AI Optimization surfaces that have not yet been verified from actual intended payload output.

In particular, this affects promotion of families inferred from docs-only coverage where the real payloads have not yet been pulled under the intended workflow.

This does not erase:

- already captured playground-based understanding
- the Firecrawl portion of Batch H
- the authority already landed for provider admission and VEDA ownership posture

---

## Current repo reading

The repo should currently be read as follows:

- Firecrawl-related Batch H work may continue from the existing grounding set
- DataForSEO AI-surface family promotion should remain conservative
- docs-confirmed but unsampled DataForSEO structures should not be promoted as fully governed canonical family definitions yet
- deferred-owned posture remains the safe default where real payload confirmation is still missing

---

## Resume condition

Resume the paused DataForSEO portion of Batch H when provider credits are available and real payloads can be pulled for the still-unsampled AI-surface endpoints used in the intended workflow.

At that point, the next step is:

1. capture real payloads
2. inventory the payloads from actual output
3. compare those payloads against the current Batch H design/inventory notes
4. then promote only the now-evidenced structures into authority docs

---

## What not to do during the pause

- do not promote docs-only DataForSEO structures as if they are payload-confirmed
- do not invent canonical families to avoid the credit gate
- do not treat the pause as a reason to reopen settled provider-admission posture
- do not force Batch H to completion by pretending unsampled payloads are fully known
